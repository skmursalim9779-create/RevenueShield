# 📌 Project Overview

**Revenue Shield** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Predictive Analytics platform designed to help hotel businesses understand, predict, and act on booking cancellation risk.

Traditional dashboards usually answer:

> **“What happened?”**

Revenue Shield attempts to continue the analysis:

> **What happened → Why did it happen → What is likely to happen → How much value is at risk → What action should be taken?**

The platform combines:

- Business Intelligence
- Exploratory Data Analysis
- Revenue Analytics
- Feature Engineering
- Supervised Machine Learning
- Risk Scoring
- Business-oriented Explainability
- Revenue-at-Risk estimation
- Decision automation
- Optional Generative AI

The central business workflow is:

```text
DATA
  ↓
INFORMATION
  ↓
INSIGHT
  ↓
DECISION
  ↓
ACTION
